Kentucky Straight Bourbon Whiskey

Seven years,
one barrel.

Most bourbon on a shelf is blended from hundreds of barrels so that this year tastes like last year. This is the opposite of that: one cask, emptied once, bottled at the proof it reached on its own.

Tasting

What it does in the cup.

Nose

Baked apple and clove, then old leather and a rickhouse in August. Given a minute in the cup, dark honey underneath.

Palate

Arrives hot and settles sweet — toasted pecan, brown butter, a seam of dry oak tannin holding the whole thing straight.

Finish

Long, warm, faintly peppery, with the char coming back at the end. Water opens the fruit; it does not need much.

Specification

The numbers on the back label.

How to drink it

Three ways, in order of how much we mean it.

  1. 01

    Neat, in pewter

    The cup takes the heat off. Give it two minutes before the first sip.

  2. 02

    With a few drops of water

    At 120 proof, water is not a compromise. A quarter teaspoon opens the apple and the honey.

  3. 03

    One large cube

    If you must. It closes the nose but keeps the finish.
