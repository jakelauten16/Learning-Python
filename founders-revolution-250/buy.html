Before you order

How it ships.

  1. 01

    Shipping is flat-rate

    Up to four bottles ship for $19.99; five or six for $29.99. Rates are set by Kentucky Bourbon Direct and shown at checkout.

  2. 02

    An adult over 21 must sign

    Age is verified at checkout and again on delivery. Someone 21 or older has to be there to sign for it.

  3. 03

    Not every state

    Spirits cannot be shipped everywhere. The store will tell you at checkout whether it can reach your address.
