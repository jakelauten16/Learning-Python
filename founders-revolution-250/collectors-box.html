The 250
Collector’s Box

A seven-year aged, cask strength Kentucky straight bourbon, presented in a handcrafted box honoring America’s Founders and the 250th anniversary of the nation. Every set is numbered. When the barrel is empty, it is finished.

Contents

Five pieces, one number.

Each item in the box carries the same set number as the certificate. Nothing in it is sold separately.

  1. 01

    The whiskey

    750 ml of single barrel, seven-year, cask strength Kentucky straight bourbon. Uncut and non-chill filtered, at 120 proof. The neck tag carries your set number.

  2. 02

    Handcrafted engraved presentation box

    Solid hardwood with a hinged lid, engraved with the Heritage Select seal and the eagle. Inside, a fitted insert holds each piece in its own recess so nothing moves in transit.

  3. 03

    Pewter eagle bottle stopper

    Cast in pewter and hand-finished, with a cork shank sized to the bottle neck. It takes over once the original cork has done its work.

  4. 04

    Two pewter Jefferson cups

    The straight-sided beaker Jefferson kept at Monticello. Pewter pulls heat from your hand into the whiskey, which takes the edge off a barrel-proof pour better than glass does.

  5. 05

    Numbered certificate of authenticity

    Signed, dated, and matched to the number on the bottle. It records the barrel the whiskey came from and the day it was drawn.
