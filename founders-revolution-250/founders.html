About

Meet the founders

Two men who traced their own families back to the Revolution, and then went looking for a way to mark its two hundred and fiftieth year.

BL
Co-founder

Dr. Bruce Lautenschlager

Bruce is an inventor, author and entrepreneur, and has been involved in many businesses over the years. As a history buff, he was aware of the Sons of the American Revolution. To become a member his genealogy had to be traced and proven to show he was related to a Revolutionary patriot. In April 2025 he was inducted into the John Martin Chapter of his local SAR.

As the SAR celebrates our nation’s Revolutionary history and honours the patriots who served and secured our freedom, Bruce wanted to find his own way to commemorate them. With the nation’s 250-year celebration beginning in 2026, he felt inspired to profile, celebrate and recognise those patriots.

AH
Co-founder

Allen Hayne

Allen co-founded this business to honour his ancestors who fought in the Revolutionary War, and to celebrate the 250th anniversary of the founding of the United States. For Allen, bourbon is more than a spirit — it is a vessel for heritage, history and gratitude toward the freedoms we enjoy as Americans.

Before entering the world of bourbon, Allen dedicated 39 years to education in Indiana. He served as a teacher, swimming coach, Dean of Students, principal, and ultimately retired as a school superintendent. His personal connection to American history runs deep, with multiple relatives traced back to the Revolutionary War. He is also an active member of the Sons of the American Revolution, carrying forward his family’s legacy of patriotism.

Why the release exists

A glass raised to the people who made it possible.

Between them, Bruce and Allen have more relatives on the Revolutionary rolls than they have bottles in this release. That is roughly the point of it.
