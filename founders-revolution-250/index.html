The Bourbon

Seven years, one barrel, no adjustments.

Most bourbon on a shelf is blended from hundreds of barrels to taste the same every year. This is the opposite of that: one cask, emptied once, bottled at the proof it reached on its own.

Nose

Baked apple and clove, then old leather and a rickhouse in August. Given a minute, dark honey.

Palate

Arrives hot and settles sweet — toasted pecan, brown butter, a seam of dry oak holding it straight.

Finish

Long, warm, faintly peppery. Water opens the fruit; it does not need much.
