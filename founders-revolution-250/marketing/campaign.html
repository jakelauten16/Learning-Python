Read this first

Four constraints that shape everything

These are not cautions, they are rules that decide what the campaign can be. Two of them will change how you spend money.

TikTok: no paid alcohol ads. Anywhere.

Alcohol is a globally prohibited advertising category on TikTok. There is no age-gate, no exemption, no spend level that unlocks it. Your TikTok presence is organic only.

Organic brand accounts for spirits do exist, but TikTok also restricts content depicting alcohol consumption. So the TikTok posts below are built around history, craft and objects — the barrel, the cup, the certificate, the genealogy. Nobody drinks on camera.

Meta: allowed, but gated

Facebook and Instagram permit alcohol advertising with restrictions. You must set 21+ age targeting in the US, exclude countries where alcohol ads are banned, and set an age restriction on the Page itself so the organic feed is gated too.

Do the Page-level age gate before your first post. Retrofitting it after you have an audience loses you reach.

You cannot imply SAR or DAR endorse this

Bruce and Allen are members, and you support these organisations. That is a true and powerful story. "The official bourbon of the SAR" is not, and neither is anything that reads that way.

Every line below is written as membership and support, never as endorsement. Keep it that way — a lineage society that feels used will say so loudly, to exactly the audience you are courting.

TTB and CAN-SPAM apply

No health claims, no claims about the benefits of consumption, and no strength claims framed as a selling point beyond the factual proof statement. Age and proof statements must be accurate — yours are.

Every commercial email needs a real physical postal address and a working one-click unsubscribe. Do not buy a list. See the email section for how to build one legitimately.

Positioning

Why this brand has an unfair advantage

Most craft bourbons launch with a good liquid and no audience. You have the opposite problem, which is a much better problem.

The number means something

250 sets for 250 years is not a marketing device, it is the edition size. Scarcity that carries an argument sells itself; scarcity for its own sake reads as a tactic. Never explain the number without the year attached to it.

Your buyers are already organised

The SAR has roughly 500+ chapters and the DAR is far larger. These are people who have documented descent from a Revolutionary patriot, who meet monthly, who publish newsletters, and who are actively planning America 250 events. Most brands never find an audience this well matched. You can address it by chapter.

The founders are the proof

A 39-year school superintendent and an inventor who traced their own genealogies to Revolutionary patriots is not a brand story, it is two people's actual lives. Lead with them. It is the one thing a competitor cannot copy.

The 250th did not end on 4 July

The Fourth has passed. Anything framed as a countdown to it is now wrong — the release is "for the 250th year", not "before the Fourth", which is a longer and more durable claim anyway.

The Revolution's 250th anniversaries run to 2033 — Yorktown in 2031, the Treaty of Paris in 2033 — and lineage-society chapters programme against that whole arc. The nearest peak is Constitution Week, 17–23 September, an observance the DAR itself petitioned Congress for in 1955. September is also National Bourbon Heritage Month. See the September plan for the day-by-day.

Chapters plan months ahead and set their programme when they resume in September, so officer outreach has to land at the start of a season, not the week of an event.

Email

Four audiences, four different letters

The single biggest mistake here would be sending one blast to everybody. A chapter registrar and a bourbon reviewer want opposite things from you.

Do not buy a list

A purchased list will burn your sending domain inside a week and exposes you under CAN-SPAM. Build it properly instead:

  • Chapters: SAR and DAR publish officer contacts publicly. Write to them individually, by hand, as a member. This is relationship outreach, not bulk mail — 40 good letters beat 4,000 sends.
  • Consumers: a capture form on the site. That list is opt-in and yours forever.
  • Warm the domain before any volume: SPF, DKIM and DMARC set, then ramp over two to three weeks. Skipping this is how new domains land in spam permanently.

Alcohol and raffles: before you offer a set for a chapter raffle or auction, check that state's rules. Raffling alcohol needs a specific permit in most states and is flatly illegal in some. A chapter would not thank you for the problem.

Capabilities

What you'd have to do to let Claude run this

You asked what it takes. Here is the honest answer, including the parts that are not currently possible.

Email — this one works

Klaviyo and Mailchimp both have Claude connectors. Connect one at claude.ai → Settings → Connectors, then enable it for the chat you are working in. After that Claude can draft campaigns straight into your account, pull list and performance data, and revise from real results.

You never paste an API key into a chat. The connector handles authentication itself.

Social — this one does not

There is no connector that posts organic content to Facebook, Instagram or TikTok. The social entries in the directory are analytics (Supermetrics, Windsor.ai, vidIQ) or paid-ads (AdWhispr). None of them publishes a post.

So social stays copy-and-paste, or goes through a scheduler like Buffer or Metricool that you paste into. That is a real limit, not a setup step you are missing.

  1. Age-restrict the Facebook Page and the Instagram account first

    Before the first post. Meta Business Suite → Settings → Page restrictions → 21+ for the US. Retrofitting this after you have followers costs you reach.

  2. Set the sending domain up properly

    SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the domain you will send from, then warm it over two to three weeks before any volume. This is the single highest-consequence technical step in the whole campaign.

  3. Connect Klaviyo or Mailchimp

    claude.ai → Settings → Connectors. Then turn it on for the chat. Tell me when it is live and I will load these sequences in as drafts.

  4. Put a capture form on the site

    You have traffic arriving at a page with no way to stay in touch. One field and a 21+ checkbox on the buy page is worth more over a year than any single post here.

  5. Decide who approves

    You chose draft-only, which is right. Name one person who reviews the queue and how often. An approval step nobody owns becomes an auto-post step by accident.

What Claude still cannot do, with or without connectors

  • Post to Facebook, Instagram or TikTok. No connector exists.
  • Run unattended on a schedule from this session. This is a temporary container that gets wiped. Recurring work needs a host — GitHub Actions, a server, or a scheduling SaaS. You chose to skip that for now.
  • Find or verify contact lists. Chapter officers must be gathered from the organisations' own published directories, by a person.
  • Approve its own outbound. By your instruction and by good sense, everything above is a draft until you say otherwise.
