September 2026 · American Founders Heritage Select 250

The month the
calendar hands you

September is National Bourbon Heritage Month and it contains Constitution Week — an observance the Daughters of the American Revolution created themselves. For a bourbon made by two lineage-society members, those two things landing in the same thirty days is the best gift the calendar has to offer.

Bourbon is America’s native spirit by act of Congress. Say so, in the month Congress set aside for it.

A correction to the first playbook

That playbook told you to work backwards to 4 July 2026. That date has passed — it was 57 days ago. Anything still framed as a countdown to the Fourth is now wrong, and I have fixed it there too.

The America 250 story is not over, though, and that matters for how you talk about the number. The Revolution’s 250th anniversaries run to 2033 — Yorktown in 2031, the Treaty of Paris in 2033 — and lineage-society chapters are programming against that whole arc. So the release stops being "before the Fourth" and becomes "for the 250th year", which is a longer and more durable claim anyway.

Two dates that need care, not enthusiasm

  • Friday 11 September. Post remembrance or post nothing. No product, no link, no purchase language, no branded hashtag. Given your Responder Bourbon and Project Never Broken partnerships a first-responder note is authentic — but commercial messaging on 9/11 draws real backlash, and it deserves to.

FB-S1Tue 1 Sept · Heritage Month opener
In 1964, Congress passed a resolution declaring bourbon whiskey "a distinctive product of the United States." Not a style. Not a tradition. A product of this country, recognised by act of Congress. In 2007 the Senate went further and set aside September as National Bourbon Heritage Month. That resolution is worth reading, because of how it ends — it asks Americans who enjoy bourbon to do so "responsibly and in moderation." We will drink to that. American Founders Heritage Select 250 is a seven-year Kentucky straight bourbon, drawn from a single barrel and bottled at cask strength — 120 proof, uncut and non-chill filtered. 250 sets exist, one for each year of the republic. When the barrel is empty, it is finished. Happy Bourbon Heritage Month. 🔗 foundersrevolution250.kybourbondirect.com Must be 21 or older. Please enjoy responsibly.
Why this opens the month: it earns the patriotic frame with a fact instead of a flag, and it quotes the moderation line before anyone can ask you to. Image: the bottle on the bar.
FB-S2Mon 7 Sept · Labor Day · the makers
A bourbon is not made by a brand. It is made by people whose names are not on the label. The barrel came from a cooperage — T&P Hooper. Raising a barrel is a trade you learn from someone who already knows it, and it has not changed much in two hundred and fifty years. The box came from Deer Ridge Woodcraft, cut and engraved and fitted so that five pieces sit in it without moving. On the day this country sets aside for working people, those are the ones worth naming. Must be 21 or older. Please enjoy responsibly.
Tag both partners. Labour Day content from brands is usually a sale; naming your suppliers instead is the opposite move and reads as such.

Act II · 17–23 September · the peak

Constitution Week

The single best-matched week in the year for this brand. Everything in Act I is setup for these four posts.

FB-S4Thu 17 Sept · Constitution Day · flagship
Two hundred and thirty-nine years ago today, in a room in Philadelphia with the windows shut against the September heat and the eavesdroppers, thirty-nine men signed the Constitution of the United States. Forty-two were present. Three refused. That is the part worth remembering — that it was argued over, that men who had fought the same war disagreed to the end about what should replace the king, and that the document survived the argument. It has been amended twenty-seven times since. It was built to be. Constitution Week runs today through the 23rd. It exists because the Daughters of the American Revolution petitioned Congress for it in 1955, and President Eisenhower signed it into law the following year — Public Law 915. Our founders are members of the Sons of the American Revolution, and both had to document their own descent from a patriot before they could join. That is why this bourbon exists, and why there are exactly 250 sets of it. Read the thing this week. It is four pages. Must be 21 or older. Please enjoy responsibly.
Your best post of the year. "Read the thing this week, it is four pages" is the line people will quote back. Note: DAR created the week — that is history, not endorsement. Do not add a partnership claim.

FB-S5Tue 22 Sept · the signers
Of the thirty-nine men who signed the Constitution, one had signed the Declaration eleven years earlier and would live to see the country he argued into existence reach its fiftieth year. Several were bankrupt within a decade. One was killed in a duel. They were not a monument. They were a committee that disagreed loudly and got something done anyway. This is Constitution Week — it runs through tomorrow. If you have never read the document straight through, this is the week. Must be 21 or older. Please enjoy responsibly.
Verify the specifics before posting if you name individuals — this copy stays general on purpose. Franklin signed both; Hamilton died in the duel in 1804; Robert Morris was imprisoned for debt. Name them only if you want to check each.

FB-S6Thu 24 Sept · the box as an object

The rest of it does not. Somebody will still have the cups in fifty years, and the number will still say which of the 250 it was. That is a harder thing to buy than it sounds. The set — $250. Before 1 September

What has to be true first

  1. Age-restrict the Facebook Page and Instagram account

    21+ for the US, before the first post goes up. This is the one item that cannot be fixed later without losing reach.

  2. Put your postal address into the email footer

    Legally required on every commercial email under CAN-SPAM, along with a working unsubscribe. The bracketed placeholders in the letters are there for it.

  3. Gather chapter contacts by hand this week

    SAR and DAR publish officer contacts. A-1 goes out Tuesday and it is a personal letter from Bruce, not a mail-merge — 40 careful sends, one follow-up, never two.

  4. Confirm the [N] remaining and the Terre Haute giving claim

    Both appear in copy this month. Neither should go out unverified.

  5. Decide the 11 September call now, not on the day

    Remembrance post or silence. Both are right. Deciding it under time pressure on the morning is how a brand ends up posting a discount code.

  6. Add a capture form to the site

    Two of this month's five sends go to a consumer list. If that list is empty, this is the fortnight to start it.
