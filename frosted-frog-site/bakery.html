How it started

The Frosted Frog began the way most good things do, with a birthday cake for someone I love, and then a request for another one. A neighbor asked for cookies. A friend asked for a shower. Somewhere in there it stopped being a favor and started being a bakery.

I grew up baking beside somebody who never measured anything and never missed, and most of what I know came from standing at that counter. The rest came from a lot of Saturdays, a lot of buttercream, and a few cakes that went straight in the bin before anybody saw them.

Why a frog

Because the first cake I ever decorated for someone else had a frog on it, in green buttercream, for a five-year-old who wanted nothing else. It was not elegant. He loved it. The name stuck, and the frog has been piping ever since.

What I Believe

How this kitchen works

Small batches, on purpose

One week's orders at a time. Nothing is frozen ahead, nothing sits in a case, and when the week is full it's full, I'd rather turn an order away than hand you something rushed.

Real ingredients, no shortcuts

Butter, not shortening. Real vanilla, real chocolate, real fruit. Buttercream made the slow way because you can taste the difference, and so can your guests.

Made for your table

Every order is baked for a specific person and a specific day. I'd rather ask three questions about your party than guess and get it close.

Honest about the kitchen

This is a home kitchen, not a factory. It's licensed as a cottage bakery, I'll always tell you plainly what shares a counter with what, and I'll never promise an allergen-free product I can't guarantee.

Fair, clear prices

What you see is what it costs. No card fees added on, no surprise upcharges, you get a total before you ever pay a cent.

Hand-finished, every one

Piped by hand, iced one at a time, dipped the same afternoon they're rolled. The detail work is the whole point; it's the part that doesn't scale and the part I won't give up.
