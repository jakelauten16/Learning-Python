Our Story

A home kitchenwith very high standards

The Frosted Frog started the way most good things do — with a birthday cake for someone we love, and then a request for another one.

Today it's a licensed cottage bakery run out of our own kitchen: small batches, real butter, and the kind of detail work that doesn't scale — hand-piped buttercream, iced cookies done one at a time, cake pops dipped and dried the same afternoon they're rolled.

Because everything is made to order, nothing sits in a case. What you pick up was baked for you, that week, on purpose.
