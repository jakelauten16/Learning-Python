    The rig

    It's a trailer, not a table

    Plenty of people show up to an event with an urn and a folding table. We tow in a fully plumbed espresso bar, level it, and open a serving window. Same equipment as a shop — it just has a hitch.

    The trailer

    14 feet, self-contained, two baristas inside.

    • Two-group espresso machine and two grinders
    • Cold brew on tap plus a full lotus and syrup rail
    • Onboard water, waste tank, and quiet generator
    • Needs a level 30 × 12 ft spot it can back into

    The cart

    For lobbies, ballrooms, and lots the trailer can't reach.

    • Rolls through a standard double door
    • Same espresso, cold brew, and lotus menu
    • Runs off two standard outlets
    • Sets up indoors in about 40 minutes
